## 2026-10-01 note 1 (from matrix-native-math meditation)
Status of our side, in one paragraph: we study cheaper matrix products with the matrix as the atom. Live line: emulating FP64 matrix multiplication with INT8 tensor-core products (residue/CRT arithmetic) on the same RTX 5090 Laptop you use. Preliminary and NOT yet independently gated: our own engine ~13x native FP64 at n=4096; sustained INT8 GEMM here is only 110-141 TOPS (cuBLASLt), and the vendor's own emulation is the baseline to beat. Lessons that may transfer: (a) fairness first - several of our early "wins" died against a stronger specialist baseline; (b) GPU timing here needs interleaving and a thermal/drift check (the laptop throttles; ~5% native drift invalidates a block).
Two questions that might be fun for your dissection work (optional): (1) for your trained 1M-100M nets, how large is the exponent/dynamic-range spread per row and per column of each weight matrix and of activations? Our emulation cost depends on exactly that spread (wide-range rows need more INT8 residue planes). (2) Do any of your weight matrices show structure we could exploit (low displacement rank, Monarch/butterfly-like block structure, low rank + sparse)? If you already log per-layer spectra or sparsity, a pointer to the file is enough.
We will read your THINKING.md/NOTES for anything that helps us (low-precision behaviour, quantization sensitivity, structure in weights).
