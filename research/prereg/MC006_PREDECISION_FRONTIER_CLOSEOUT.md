## Purpose

MC006 is the main knowledge-like branch in the current atlas: real capital
facts versus fictional/task-local overwrites. The useful question is no longer
"can we find another perfect hidden separator?" The V14-V28 chain already shows
that hidden monitors can exist while output/candidate geometry, steering
failure, shuffle nulls, and transfer fragility block mechanism promotion.

This closeout turns that chain into an executable decision:

> Is MC006 a hidden-state-allowed predecision route, or a monitor-only
> decision-timing diagnostic?

## Decision Rules

Promotion rule:

> Promote MC006 to hidden-state intervention work only if the behavior substrate
> exists, an early monitor exists, and candidate/final-output geometry,
> selected-search shuffle nulls, steering stress, and transfer-bank gates no
> longer explain or block the route.

Bound rule:

> Bound MC006 as monitor-only if early signals exist but final/candidate
> geometry, shuffle nulls, steering failure, and transfer-bank insufficiency
> still block actionable control.

Kill rule:

> Kill final-position and ordinary delayed-city repair attempts if the route
> remains output/candidate-visible or transfer-bank-insufficient after V14-V28.

Containment rule:

> The allowed claim is decision timing for capital-fact override behavior, not a
> truth, knowledge, honesty, or factuality vector.

Export rule:

> Export `FINAL_STATE_OUTPUT_VISIBLE`, `PREDECISION_MONITOR_NO_LEVER`,
> `CANDIDATE_DECOUPLED_SHUFFLE_OVERFIT`, and
> `TRANSFER_ROLE_REPAIR_BANK_INSUFFICIENT` as the active diagnostics.

## Forbidden Moves

- Do not steer V15/V16/V25 hidden directions as if they passed mechanism gates.
- Do not treat a perfect hidden AUC as interesting without output/candidate,
  shuffle, and transfer controls.
- Do not continue ordinary delayed-city prompt repairs as promotion attempts.
- Do not call the MC006 route a knowledge mechanism.
