## LAW 1 — EVERY DEATH IS A STEP-DEATH [invariant]

With the training stream paused, an established write's read drifts EXACTLY 0.0 (e283).
Nothing fades, leaks, or decays. Every memory death ever observed in this lab is caused
by optimizer steps: stepped on, steered past, or steered into (e273/e278/e283/e285/x14).

*Scope:* none. The oldest law; never dented. [The mechanism noun — whether all step-one
