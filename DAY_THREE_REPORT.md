  report revealed the deeper dissociation: compatibility moved WITHOUT
  alignment.
- **e052 (zero-GPU reanalysis):** the graft-damage assay was dominated
  by host-side own-organ load (r = 0.807); the LN-calibration
  alternative died; a real L2-localized geometry residue survived

  **VISIBILITY-LIMITED: the stream basis is closed to evolution at this
  regime.** Written once at init; not rewritten by surgery, regime, or
  selection.

- **The shape:** a last-~7-token spike (up to +3.2 nats/position) + a

- **The negative-utility finding:** 13-32% of old positions IMPROVE
