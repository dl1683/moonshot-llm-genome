## 1. THE TERRAIN STORY — Fig-5 is a three-organism stitch in mixed currencies,
   and its middle band is not a terrain measurement at all

T144's figure plan: "three terrains of increasing width — the raw-gradient
ray kills at 0.92; the sign-normalized ray's at ~2.5 (opt1); random rays'
at 4-10x (g3K)." Attack each band:

**(1a) The middle band is a trajectory number pasted onto a terrain axis.**
The g-ray 0.92 is a STATIC endpoint distance (e191, graded grid, cliff-edge
bracket [0.80, 0.92]). The random band 4-10x is STATIC endpoint distance
(g3K iso rungs). The "sign-ray 2.5" is NEITHER: it is Adam's CUMULATIVE
PATH LENGTH over two adapting steps (1.654 + 1.091 = 2.489, A0), where the
direction is sign(g_t) RECOMPUTED at step 2. No static sign(g_0)-ray has
ever been mapped on e131 — the curve has exactly ONE static-equivalent
point (step 1, pre-adaptation: gm12 0.678 at D 1.654, where the g-ray
reads 0.0007 — good evidence the ordering is real, but one point is not a
width), and no endpoint-distance read exists for the two-step kill
(u_cos between Adam steps 1-2 unreported; endpoint <= 2.489). A figure
whose x-axis is "distance from root at which the ray kills" currently
plots one ray, one point, and one path length.

**(1b) The third band is a different organism with a different ruler, a
different fact, and wash-anchored units.** g3K's random kill rungs are on
the g3 composed organism (0.87M params, e098/s4305 lineage), read on the
host's S-DISC discriminative battery — not the e131 ZEPHYRA install (2.74M
params, e048/e131 lineage, g-12 battery). The rungs are multiples of the
g3 organism's OWN wash-1x (D = 0.916/0.926), i.e., the ruler is "its own
Adam step," not absolute L2. In absolute L2 the band is ~3.7-14.8 — its
lower edge nearly touches Adam's 2.49. And in PER-COORDINATE RMS (the
Rule-12 currency): e131 dies at 0.92/sqrt(2.74M) = 5.6e-4 (g-ray) and
2.49/sqrt(2.74M) = 1.5e-3 (sign path), while g3's random kill at rung 8
is ~7.8e-3 — the ordering survives, but only because BOTH sides of the
comparison were lucky enough to preserve it under a currency the figure
never states. A 3.2x dimension ratio is doing unacknowledged work between
panels two and three.

**(1c) What licenses the picture.** A single-organism, single-ruler,
single-currency map: on the e131 root, static graded jumps along (i) the
g-ray (committed — e191, reuse), (ii) the STATIC sign(g_0) ray, (iii) 3
fresh random Gaussian rays; same D grid, same g-12 battery + CE_R, both
currencies reported (absolute L2 and per-coordinate RMS). Eval-only, CPU,
same machinery as e191 — minutes of compute (see FORCED EXPERIMENT). Until
then, T144's "lethality ordering stands on mapped ground" is true for one
and one-half of its three bands; R58 3e's "unified displacement table owed
before any sentence spans both organisms" is still owed, and Fig-5 as
planned would inherit the debt at figure grade.

## 2. THE PRE-REGISTRATION PARADOX — the map discipline is working; the
   branch design conflates hypothesis space with outcome space

Score the day: e188's map fired (Branch B, against its own favorite — the
discipline's best case); opt1b CAP-NEITHER (no bar); opt1c
KILL-OUT-OF-WINDOW (no bar — "the third reading is the finding"); e191
STATIC-CLIFF fired. Two of four runs landed outside their named branches.
That is not failure — no bar shopping occurred, graded outcomes were
reported verbatim, follow-ups were dispatched — but note WHY opt1c's
branches missed: the window [2.12, 3.27] IS the hypothesis. It is A0's own
kill 2.4893 ±15% (R58 1c: circular by construction for A0; the only
independent bracket, A3's, is [1.45, 3.64]). A map whose window is derived
from the arm that instantiates "direction doesn't matter" cannot help but
register a direction-dependent outcome as a third outcome. The paradox is
an artifact of windows-as-hypotheses.

**Amendment (proposed, two clauses):**
- **Maps carry a named third branch by default** — not just the structural
  "fires no bar" opt1c inherited from opt1b, but a pre-registered
  OUT-OF-WINDOW branch split by SIDE: below-window and above-window get
  separate one-line interpretations at registration time (below = direction
  carries lethality; above = direction carries protection). opt1c's result
  would then have been "the below-window branch fired" — a confirmation
  with a name, not an anomaly without one.
- **Windows derived from the same arm the hypothesis names are stamped
  circular at registration** (R58 1c made this finding; make it a rule so
  the next map cannot inherit the stain silently). GRADED-MAP stays as the
  floor: any partial pattern, curve verbatim.

Every pump reading is the same object: the e131_consolidated_e113 root,

> "On one organism (2.7M-param consolidated host, single install lineage,

> 0.94-0.96 at D 0.2-0.37 vs root 0.916) followed by a cliff at D ~0.9;
> no second organism, second fact, or non-gradient ray has been mapped."

The forced experiment's random-ray legs fill the ray-family hole for free
