## Claim 1 — THE NO-BASIN TRAJECTORY LAW (T137/g3K, newest)

Claim: no memory state survives continued training on any stream with the fact's
windows absent; STATIC random displacement shows a graded 4-10x basin (kappa ~5-6 at
matched per-coordinate RMS) — forgetting is trajectory-typed, not displacement-typed.

### (a) Closest works
1. **Frankle, Dziugaite, Roy, Carbin — "Linear Mode Connectivity and the Lottery
   Ticket Hypothesis" (ICML 2020, arXiv:1912.05671)**, with Garipov et al. 2018 and
   Draxler et al. 2018 (mode connectivity): SGD solutions from a shared init are
   linearly connected in low loss, while paths to/from unrelated (random) points hit
   barriers — i.e., trained points are special relative to random points in weight
   space. Opposite polarity to ours: they connect two trained endpoints; we show a
   learned PATH kills a fact where a random JUMP of equal L2 does not.
2. **Michel, Salesky, Neubig — "Regularizing Trajectories to Mitigate Catastrophic
   Forgetting" (ICLR 2020 submission, OpenReview Hkl4EANFDH; ~3 citations, not
   accepted)**: the only title-level "trajectory" prior found — argues forgetting is
   a property of the optimization TRAJECTORY and regularizes it (co-natural
   gradient). A mitigation method; no static random-displacement control; no law.
3. **Evron, Weinberger, Soudry — "How Catastrophic Can Catastrophic Forgetting Be in
   Linear Regression?" (COLT 2022, arXiv:2205.09588)** and **Goldfarb & Hand —
   "Analysis of Catastrophic Forgetting for Random Orthogonal Transformation Tasks
   in the Overparameterized Regime" (AISTATS 2023, arXiv:2207.06475)**: theory showing
   forgetting is governed by task/gradient GEOMETRY (alignment of task subspaces),
   not by how far weights move — the nearest conceptual statement of
   direction-not-distance, in linear/overparameterized theory.
4. **Mirzadeh, Farajtabar, Pascanu, Ghasemzadeh — "Understanding the Role of
   Training Regimes in Continual Learning" (NeurIPS 2020, arXiv:2006.06958)**:
   dropout/lr-decay/batch-size/width widen task-1 minima and reduce forgetting — the
   basin/displacement view of protection; never separates learned-trajectory kills
   from static displacement. (Ramasesh, Dyer, Raghu — "Anatomy of Catastrophic
   Forgetting: Hidden Representations and Task Semantics", ICLR 2021,
   arXiv:2007.07400: deeper layers and task relatedness set forgetting severity —
   representation-level, again all under real training.)

### (b) What is different here
One sentence: nobody runs the three-way controlled dissociation — continued training
(corpus OR noise-label trajectories, e185) at per-coordinate-RMS-matched displacement
kills the fact, a static Gaussian jump to the same displacement leaves it alive in a
graded 4-10x basin — and no prior work states or tests the census form "no memory
state survives continued training while surviving matched static displacement."

### (c) Named risk
**"Corollary-of-mode-connectivity" attack**: a reviewer cites Garipov/Frankle + the
Evron/Goldfarb theory line and says "trained directions vs random directions behaving
differently is known since 2018; your law is a special case." Pre-empt in the paper's
T137 clause (evidence gap 10) by stating: LMC concerns global loss along linear
interpolation between endpoints, not fact-readout survival under actual training vs a
single static jump; the graded static basin (kappa ~5-6) is itself a new quantitative
object; and the e185 noise-label arm makes it LEARNING, not corpus content, that kills.

## Claim 2 — THE WALL (g1bR/T133): commit-and-project L2 ball

Claim: one commit event + one geometric constraint (projection onto an L2 ball,
zero new parameters) holds the fact's battery-channel at ~0.9 through a +300 wash
that kills controls in 2 steps, at +0.53 nats standing CE tax; n=3 wash seeds.

### (a) Closest works
1. **Kirkpatrick et al. — EWC (PNAS 2017)**: quadratic Fisher-weighted PENALTY
   anchoring weights near the old optimum — the canonical penalty pole; soft, needs
   Fisher estimates, no guarantee.
2. **Wołczyk et al. — "Continual Learning with Guarantees via Weight Interval Bound
   Propagation" (ICML 2022, arXiv:2206.07996, InterContiNet)**: HARD constraint —
   parameters confined to nested hyperrectangles containing previous tasks' valid
   regions, with provable forgetting bounds. The strongest prior that "a hard
   constraint region protects old tasks" is a known, formalized idea.
3. **Elsayed et al. — "Weight Clipping for Deep Continual and Transfer Learning"
   (RLC 2024)**: compares hard weight-clipping constraints against L2/L2-to-init
   penalties — the closest simple hard-constraint-vs-penalty comparison. [Abstract
   via search only — FLAGGED for full-text read.]
4. **Projection family**: OGD (Farajtabar et al., AISTATS 2020), GPM (Saha et al.,
   ICLR 2021), OWM (Zeng et al., Nature Comms 2019), GEM/A-GEM (Lopez-Paz & Ranzato
   NeurIPS 2017; Chaudhry et al. 2019) — project GRADIENTS or updates onto subspaces
   orthogonal to old-task statistics; all need old-task gradients, activation
   matrices, or episodic memory buffers.

### (b) What is different here
One sentence: the wall is the MINIMAL member of the constraint family — one commit
event plus one scalar radius (no Fisher, no subspace, no buffer, no per-task
statistics, zero new parameters) — and the claim is not "constraints work" but the
quantified survival curve (flat ~0.9 through the wash that kills controls in 2 steps,
n=3, radius ~0.28x the basin prior's low end) at a measured standing CE tax.

### (c) Named risk
**"Known method class" attack**: a reviewer cites Wołczyk 2022 (guarantees!) and
Elsayed 2024 and says the wall is weight clipping rediscovered, weaker (no
guarantees, radius-tuned at R=0.7 specifically, W2/W3 order-with-R n=1). Pre-empt:
cite both explicitly as the family we minimize; the paper's object is the minimal
element + the wash-survival assay + the tax, and the honest scoping sentence (g1bR
report-scope) is already written.

## Claim 3 — THE RHYTHM (g2d/T131/T132/T136): zero-parameter self-timed replay organ

Claim: cue pool + onset monitor + replay gate (zero new parameters) self-times
resurrection events (20-45 band, duty ~70%); timing robust 3/3 wash seeds and 3/3
roots; amplitude root-draw-bound; the gate-disabled organ dies.

### (a) Closest works
1. **Robins 1995 (Connection Science)**: rehearsal/pseudorehearsal — the origin of
   replay-for-retention; always-on, externally scheduled.
2. **Shin et al. — Deep Generative Replay (NeurIPS 2017)**; **van de Ven, Siegelmann,
   Tolias — "Brain-inspired replay" (Nature Comms 2020)**: generative/representation
   replay grounded in complementary learning systems; replay interleaved every batch
   by design, never self-timed. (Also Rolnick et al. 2019 experience replay.)
3. **Klasson et al. — "Learn the Time to Learn: Replay Scheduling in Continual
   Learning" (arXiv:2209.08660)**: the nearest WHEN-to-replay prior — MCTS- or
   RL-LEARNED schedules allocating replay across tasks under a compute budget;
   a parameterized scheduler using task information, not an internal decay monitor.
4. **Selection/triggering neighbors**: Aljundi et al. — Maximal Interfered Retrieval
   (NeurIPS 2019): replay samples chosen by estimated interference each step;
   Schaul et al. — Prioritized Experience Replay (ICLR 2016): TD-error-prioritized
   sampling in RL. Neuroscience: Mattar & Daw (Nat Neurosci 2018) — normative
   need-x-gain (prediction-error) theory of WHICH memories replay; Schapiro et al.
   (Nat Comms 2018) — human replay prioritizes weakly-learned information.

### (b) What is different here
One sentence: the organ is a zero-parameter, self-TIMED gate — it fires only when
the model's OWN monitored readout of the fact decays past threshold (no task signal,
no learned scheduler, no per-step interference estimation), and the claim is the
robustness structure of the resulting sawtooth (timing seed- and root-robust,
amplitude honestly root-draw-bound).

### (c) Named risk
**"Thermostat/prioritized-replay" attack**: a reviewer cites Klasson 2022, MIR 2019,
Schaul 2016, and Mattar & Daw 2018 to say adaptive replay timing is established (in
brains and in buffers). Pre-empt: none of these triggers from an internal monitor of
the dying fact itself with zero learned components; and g2g's registered
self-timed-vs-thermostat-vs-fixed-period head-to-head (with the 0.693 fixed-arm
co-read disclosed, evidence gap 9) must run/shipping before "self-timed" goes
unqualified — the fixed-period co-read is the reviewer's strongest concrete number.

## Claim 4 — THE CONE (g3R/T135 + g3K/T137 amendment): directional fragility

Claim: the wash-aligned direction (and 45-degree tilts) kills at ~2x displacement;
matched-L2 isotropic needs 4-10x at the organism (24-32x store-isolated, threshold
ratio stated ~12-16x); the wash is aligned with the fact's death gradient
(cos -0.44) and drifts (cos d1..d300 ~0.13) — a drifting aligned front, not a fixed
direction.

### (a) Closest works
1. **Ilharco et al. — "Editing Models with Task Arithmetic" (ICLR 2023,
   arXiv:2212.04089)**: task vectors (theta_ft - theta_pre) negate to forget a
   behavior and add across tasks. The wash displacement is structurally a task
   vector OF THE WASH STREAM; the supervisor's question ("is the wash a negative
   task vector of the fact?") maps to a testable claim: cos(fact-install vector,
   wash vector) and whether theta_before - wash ~= fact-install removal.
2. **Yao, Gholami, Keutzer, Mahoney — "Hessian-based Analysis of Large Batch
   Training and Sharp Minima" (NeurIPS 2018, arXiv:1812.00518)** (+ Keskar 2017):
   sensitivity concentrated in a few curvature directions — the landscape pole.
3. **Li et al. — "Visualizing the Loss Landscape of Neural Nets" (NeurIPS 2018,
   arXiv:1712.09913)** and **Fort, Dziugaite et al. — "Deep learning versus kernel
   learning" (NeurIPS 2020, arXiv:1910.11210)**: random low-dimensional slices/
   directions show benign basins while trained/curvature directions do not —
   methodology-level prior that direction class changes apparent basin size.
   [No distinct "Fort & Schmitt" paper found on this topic; the supervisor's name
   maps to the Fort/Dziugaite arXiv:1910.11210 line.]
4. **Juneja et al. — "Linear Connectivity Reveals Generalization Strategies" (2022,
   arXiv:2205.12411)**: basin membership differentiates functionally distinct
   solutions — direction/basin structure carries semantic information.

### (b) What is different here
One sentence: no prior work measures a single installed FACT's survival as a
function of perturbation direction class at matched per-coordinate RMS — wash-aligned
and its tilts kill at ~2x where isotropic needs 4-10x (organism) / 24-32x
(store-isolated) — and none observes that naturally-continued training drifts along
a moving front aligned with the fact's death gradient (cos -0.44, drift cos ~0.13).

### (c) Named risk
**"Negative task vector" attack**: a reviewer cites Ilharco and says "the wash is
just the negative task vector of the fact; forgetting by aligned subtraction is
established." Pre-empt: task negation is an intervention an experimenter applies;
our object is what unguided continued training does (constructs a drifting aligned
front without ever seeing the fact), plus the graded isotropic baseline and the
organ-vs-organism split, which the merging literature never addresses. Consider
adopting "task vector" vocabulary and reporting the install-vs-wash cosine — it turns
the attack into a bridge.

## Claim 5 — THE ERROR-PLACEMENT COMPASS (paper C1)

Claim: where the fine-tune's ERROR on the fact is placed (jitter/splice/locked)
decides where support grows and which memory TYPE forms; observational then causal
(instrument-blindness exhibit 0.989/0.988 at the address the battery never read).

### (a) Closest works
1. **Xiao et al. — StreamingLLM / attention sinks (ICLR 2024, arXiv:2309.17453)**;
   Guo et al. — massive activations (arXiv:2402.17762) and active-dormant heads
   (arXiv:2410.13835); "When Attention Sink Emerges" (arXiv:2410.10781, sinks form
   EARLY in training): position-typed specialization exists and is
   training-emergent — but all at the attention/cache level, none about install-time
   error placement deciding memory type.
2. **Walmer et al. — "Teaching Matters: Investigating the Role of Supervision in
   Vision Transformers" (CVPR 2023)**: WHERE supervision lands (CLS token vs
   spatial tokens) determines which features ViTs learn — the closest
   supervision-placement prior; vision, feature-level, no memory typing, no causal
   transplant.
3. **Meng et al. — ROME (NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2202.05262)**: factual recall causally
   localizes at the last-subject-token position in mid-MLP — facts are positionally
   anchored at READ/EDIT time; says nothing about where the error sat during
   INSTALL.
4. **Complementary learning systems (McClelland et al. 1995; van de Ven 2020)**: the
   "memory types" framing (fast/slow stores) the paper already positions against
   (R3; scratch/massaction_key_lit.md; W003 narrowed to replay-only).

### (b) What is different here
One sentence: the compass is a causal INSTALL-TIME law — move the error and the
fact's support relocates and changes type (positional vs content-typed), with
necessity/sufficiency and transplant instruments — whereas sinks/Teaching
Matters/ROME document position effects at attention, supervision-feature, or
read-time levels without ever steering where a learned fact consolidates.

### (c) Named risk
**"Known position effects" attack**: a reviewer bundles sinks + Teaching Matters +
ROME and calls the compass a special case. Pre-empt (already drafted in R3): the
novelty is the CAUSAL compass + presence-typed routing + the failure-mode inversion,
not the existence of positional specialization; lead with the instrument-geometry
blindness exhibit (Rule 12) as the thing none of those works can produce.

## PASS (d) — T137's novelty gate: does "trajectory-vs-static" / "learned-vs-random
displacement" forgetting appear anywhere?

Phrasings tried (no hits that run the dissociation): "forgetting random perturbation
vs gradient updates same magnitude"; "forgetting weight displacement not sufficient
random noise same norm"; "trajectory not endpoint forgetting SGD path"; "fine-tuning
versus gaussian weight noise same L2 norm task retention"; "forgetting direction
matters random perturbation"; "learned vs random displacement". Verdict:

- **No paper found that states or runs the trajectory-vs-static dissociation**
  (learned path to displacement D kills a fact; random jump to D does not). The
  nearest objects, in order: (i) Evron COLT 2022 / Goldfarb & Hand AISTATS 2023 —
  THEORY that forgetting follows task/gradient geometry rather than displacement
  magnitude (predicts our alignment reading, never tests static noise arms);
  (ii) Michel et al. 2020 — "regularize the trajectory" as a method (no static
  control, unaccepted, ~3 citations); (iii) mode connectivity (Frankle 2020;
  Garipov/Draxler 2018) — trained points vs random points differ, via linear-path
  loss barriers between endpoints, not fact survival under training vs noise;
  (iv) EWC's Fisher weighting — implicit acknowledgment that equal-norm
  perturbations in different directions hurt differently, never tested against
  actual forgetting trajectories at matched norm. One 2026 OpenReview item (SFAO,
  "Selective Forgetting-Aware Optimization", Feb 2026) regulates gradient direction
  via cosine similarity to the "forgetting direction" — method-level, ML-engineering
  framing; FLAGGED: full text not read.
- **The phrase and the controlled experiment both appear absent.** T137's clause
  ("no memory state survives continued TRAINING; STATIC displacement shows a graded
  basin") is safe to state as new, with the theory line cited as consistent prior.

## The most dangerous overlap (honest call)

No kill-level overlap found for any claim. The single most dangerous is the STACK
around claim 1: Evron COLT 2022 / Goldfarb & Hand AISTATS 2023 state in theory that
forgetting is governed by task geometry (gradient alignment), not displacement
magnitude — a reviewer can present T137's conclusion as their corollary. The
dissociation experiment and the census are demonstrably absent from all of it, but
the paper MUST cite this line and lead with the controlled comparison, or the newest
law reads under-theorized. Runner-up: claim 2's Wołczyk/Elsayed hard-constraint
family (method-class overlap, defended by minimality + the assay).
