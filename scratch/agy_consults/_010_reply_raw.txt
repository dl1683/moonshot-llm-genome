### Q1: The Sharpest Hole in the Two-Channel Split

**The Hole: Conflating Token-Specific Logit Bias with a Global Volume Channel.**  
Calling out-of-room displacement the "volume channel (intensity/confidence/noise)" dangerously packages two fundamentally different mathematical phenomena into one label:
1. **Target-Aligned Logit Bias ([`e311`](file:///C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/AI%20Moonshots/moonshot-llm-genome/runs/e311/metrics.json)):** The 1-dim trigger in `e311` was explicitly constructed as the gradient of the host’s read ($\nabla_W \mathcal{L}_{\text{host}}$). In residual stream algebra, adding a vector aligned with the host's unembedding projection trivially acts as an additive scalar bias on the host's logit ($\Delta z_{\text{host}} > 0$). It boosted the host $+32\%$ because it was *target-specific*, not because out-of-room space is an abstract "volume knob".
2. **Isotropic Entropy Noise ([`x15`](file:///C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/AI%20Moonshots/moonshot-llm-genome/THINKING.md#L95-L128)):** The 10k-dim complement in `x15` is orthogonal to the room and carries no coherent alignment with any single token's unembedding. Scaling it moved logits $\sim 20\times$ as diffuse high-dimensional noise (top-1 $0.516$ vs ctrl $0.485$), creating neither content nor selective confidence.

A true volume/confidence channel must be *content-independent* (scaling confidence for whatever token the prompt demands, like temperature or LayerNorm gain). If `e311`'s knob is token-specific, it is not a volume channel—it is an out-of-room direct logit bias masquerading as gain because it was only tested on the host's own prompt.

*The Off-Target Specificity Probe (CPU eval, zero training, seconds):*
