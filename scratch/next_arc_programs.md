## 1. P5 — MIGRATION ANATOMY: two roads to the field, and the dream road (e119–e121)

### Organizing question

The lab now has two independently-discovered roads from address-store to field-store:
**road R (jittered replay)** — e109: 0.909 through the deleted coordinate, position
diversity beating matched mass 2-fold, new address rows grown at 121/125/133/137
(cos 0.76–0.85), held-30 generalizing, but D-all still fatal (some positional index
remains necessary); and **road E (deletion pressure)** — e083/T073: three erase
cycles, post-erase NLL 2.724→1.441→1.369, completion migrating onto position-keyed
machinery while the regrowth groove stays put (cos 0.52–0.60). e109's rescue_b
(+0.221 under matched-mass LOCKED replay) is a third, weak road: mass alone partly
works. **When the fact arrives, is it the same fact in the same store — or do the
routes leave different anatomy (different grown rows, different share boundary,
different generalization, different brake)? And is there a road the field prefers:
the net's own spontaneous replay — its dreams?**

This is T073's closing tension, promoted from a footnote war to a program: W003's
memory-theory analogy predicted consolidation-by-replay and won; T073 found
consolidation-by-erasure for free, which the analogy did NOT predict (lesion-induced
recovery is the bio-echo here). If both roads land in one store, the store is the
invariant and the roads are trajectories — a developmental law. If they land in
different stores, the "field" is a family of stores and the lab's newest noun splits.

### Why now / assets

T073 landed hours ago and explicitly frames its own texture (erase-weakening) as
"e113's consolidation story seen from the deletion side" — an assertion nobody has
tested; it is currently a metaphor with two data points from different nets and
protocols. The instruments all exist: the e109 jittered-replay protocol (≤180s), the
e044b/e083 erase-cycle machinery (~100 steps), the e113 deletion battery, the e116
row census, the e110 share measurement, the e108 V-geometry typing. What is missing
is exactly one thing: the two roads instantiated as a MATCHED pair — same net
family, same fact, same final-expression calibration — so the anatomy comparison is
apples-to-apples.

### First experiments

**e119 — the head-to-head anatomy (the experiment the completed picture begs for).**
Twin installs on same-family nets (e043 protocol on B43-class bases; or two
same-seed installs — the e098 ladder provides four more bases if needed). Route R:
jittered replay verbatim. Route E: three erase cycles verbatim. GATE: match final
expression at the trained geometry (calibrate replay steps so both arms land in the
same band; if either route fails to migrate on the twin install — protocol
fragility, the e043 lesson — PARK, do not tune ad hoc). Then the full comparative
battery, eval-only: (a) deletion hierarchy (row-129 → all-five-grown-rows → D-all)
× geometries (trained / held-30 / novel); (b) grown-row census (positions, cos
structure vs the original address); (c) brake sign at full field (address-deletion
delta — e115's dimmer); (d) share measurement (r\*(k)·k product at the fact's
collapse boundary); (e) family-cos typing of the migrated content. Free rider: the
locked-replay weak road rides as a third arm (it is e109's own control, nearly free).
**Registered prediction:** SAME-STORE — the routes match on (c) and (d) within the
per-net band and their (b) censuses overlap in position-band; DIFFERENT-STORES — ≥2
readouts dissociate (e.g., R grows new address rows where E grows none; R's store
survives scaffold loss at held-30 where E's does not; opposite brake signs).

**e120 — the dream road (the third road; the delight-first experiment).** The net is
never re-taught the fact; instead it is fine-tuned on ITS OWN free-run windows in
which the fact spontaneously appears (e048 machinery knows how to harvest
expression; GATE: measure spontaneous emission rate first — if zero, fall back to
tier-1: the fact spliced into the net's OWN generated contexts, which preserves the
question in weakened form). The positions are naturally jittered wherever the dreams
land — a diversity road whose only difference from e109's synthetic jitter is the
GENERATOR of the surrounding context. Matched-budget controls: (i) synthetic jittered
replay (e109 verbatim — teacher/protocol contexts), (ii) SIBLING-net spontaneous
emissions (matched tokens, wrong generator). Readout: through-deleted-row expression
+ e119's cheapest anatomy rung. **Registered prediction:** if own-dream ≥ synthetic
jitter per matched step, consolidation prefers self-generated contexts — W004's
fixed-point frame reaches into LEARNING dynamics (the net consolidates its own
dreams best); if sibling-dreams match own-dreams, any varied exposure does and self
plays no role in consolidation; if both dream arms fail where synthetic works, the
emission contexts are too narrow a distribution and the field needs diversity the
net does not give itself.

**e121 — the store-sharing probe (cross-fact coupling; doubles as P7's first leg).**
On one net: consolidate F1 by road R, then F2 by road E. Ablate F1's field mass at
its measured share boundary. SHARED-STORE predicts F2's expression also degrades
(coupled fate); SEGREGATED predicts F2 intact. The instrument built here is P7's
segregation assay — one rig, two programs.

### Program vs one-off

It is a PROGRAM if the routes dissociate (a taxonomy of field-stores, each with its
own laws — comparative anatomy, the lab's best genre) OR if the dream road fires
(self-reference enters learning dynamics — a new noun entirely). It closes as a
strong ONE-OFF if the routes are indistinguishable on every readout and the third
road fails: consolidation is then a single well-defined transformation, W005 closes
fully, and the paragraph is the legacy.

### One visualization

**The migration plate.** One horizontal timeline strip per road (R / E / locked /
dream), each a heatmap of the fact's mass by position-band (address row → grown
rows → field) sampled across training cycles: the fact visibly leaves its one bright
address cell and diffuses into the band. Roads stacked as rows of one plate so the
eye asks the question directly — same final picture or not? Beneath, a small
convergence scatter: (address-mass, field-mass) trajectory per road — curves
converging on one endpoint (same store) or landing on two (different stores).

## 2. P6 — SELF-AT-DISTANCE: the fixed point across runs, windows, and time (e122–e124)

### Organizing question

W004: X is self iff X looks like what my weights produce — identity is a fixed-point
property. The holographic check rejects every outsider, forged stamp included
(T062). But the frame's OWN edges are untested: is the fixed point position-free,
run-free, and TIME-free? Concretely: **does the anchor accept the same net's field
from another run at another position — and from an earlier checkpoint of itself?
How fast does yesterday's self become other?** e111 already hints the static readout
is generator-indexed (held-out same-net rows pass occupancy at 0.841 vs own 0.837),
while e099/e108's promptcopy says own weights alone are NOT self (corpus tokens
through own forward pass still collapse) — the stamp lives on the product (weights ×
self-generated statistics). Whether the DYNAMIC check (free-run acceptance) is
generator-level or episode-level is completely open, and self-through-training-time
has never been measured by anyone.

### Why now / assets

The e108 transplant rig, the binary healthy-gap readout, the k=7 subspace occupancy
instrument, and the donor battery (sibling/foreign/forged — all known-reject
calibrations) all exist. Two same-family nets with distinct runs are trivially
available. The training-time ladder needs one rebuild: per-eval snapshots exist for
final states only, so e123 re-runs the install protocol verbatim with a per-K-step
snapshot cadence (~100–200 steps, minutes) — the lab's Rule-10 habit pays for the
whole program.

### First experiments

**e122 — cross-run / cross-window self-transplant.** Net: e053c or e048_repro. Two
free-runs (different seeds/contexts) → donor band from run A spliced into run B:
first at native position (clean), then shifted (with scaffold controls — D0129/row-0
taught that whole-window scaffolding matters; include a whole-cache transplant arm
so scaffold loss is not confounded with identity rejection). Readouts: binary
healthy-gap (e108), free-run continuation, k=7 occupancy (expected to PASS per e111
— a static-pass/dynamic-reject dissociation would itself be a finding: the shadow
admits what the process rejects).
**Registered prediction:** H-FIXED-POINT-UNIVERSAL — accept (healthy gap ≈
own-native) at every position: self is a property of weights alone. H-RUN-LOCKED —
reject cross-run splices: a SECOND, episode-level layer of self (the hologram
encodes this run's interference pattern, not just this generator's statistics; W004
refined from "identity is process" to "identity is THIS episode's process").
H-WINDOW-ONLY — accept overlapping, reject disjoint windows.

**e123 — the self-drift curve (self through training time).** The snapshot ladder
from one install/consolidation run: donor field from checkpoint t−k, recipient at t,
sweep k → acceptance vs checkpoint distance. **Registered prediction:** a measurable
structure — monotone decay with a half-life (self drifts as weights move; the first
self-motion law) or a STEP (self rekeys abruptly). The cross-program rung: cross a
CONSOLIDATION event (graduate the fact mid-ladder) — if the pre-consolidation field
stops being self to the post-consolidation net, migration rekeys identity, and P5
and P6 share a mechanism claim. FLAT acceptance everywhere = self is weights-indexed
only — the frame's plainest confirmation, still a card, less a program.

**e124 — the product clause at fixed weights.** Same weights, donor fields generated
at T=0.2 / T=1.4 (and teacher-forced corpus windows — known reject): maps WHICH
statistics of the product the check reads. Runs only if e122 accepts cross-run (else
the episode layer dominates and fixed-weight regimes are moot).

### Program vs one-off

PROGRAM if run-locked (two-layer self: generator + episode — the anchor's check
becomes a hierarchy, and e112's forgery failure gets its reason: forging a generator
stamp cannot fake an episode hologram) or if drift is structured (half-life/step —
identity acquires a time constant tied to weight motion, and the
consolidation-rekeying link fires). ONE-OFF if flat-accept everywhere: W004 closes
maximally strong, and the object is done.

### One visualization

**The tissue-typing plate.** An MHC-style acceptance matrix: rows = donors (own-run
native, own-run shifted, cross-run, checkpoint t−k ladder, sibling, foreign, forged),
columns = recipients (runs / checkpoints); cell brightness = acceptance gap. The
self-block vs other-block at a glance; the checkpoint diagonal either uniformly
bright (flat, weights-indexed) or fading (half-life) or cliffed (rekeying step) —
the immunology echo made literal, as the lab's own instrument.

## 3. P7 — THE ATTACK SURFACE: forgetting a mature memory without unselfing (e125–e127)

### Organizing question

The arc's last word — the address becomes a brake, not a home — quietly inverts the
lab's day-1 edit law ("removal is surgical"). Every removal protocol the lab and the
field use is address- or representation-targeted; the mature fact lives in the
field. e113: deleting all five address rows costs nothing (0.89–0.93); e115: at full
field the address is SUPPRESSIVE — deleting it can raise expression (0.785→0.905);
T073: repeated erasure is itself a consolidation protocol — deletion pressure moves
the fact DEEPER. **So: what does remove a graduated fact? And — the sharp edge —
the field that stores the fact is the same directional field whose ~share-constant
mass sustains the run's very selfhood (e075: anchor-band removal collapses the run
into the attractor). Can a net forget a mature memory without unselfing itself?**

This is the coordinator-under-listed edge: the unlearning program (e043 surgery,
e065 RMU) never met the consolidation program (e109–e118). The five-day arc ended
by making them collide.

### Why now / assets

Graduated-fact nets exist (e109/e113 rigs; e119's twins will be matched pairs). The
share instrument (e110/e117) measures any field-mass boundary. The e075
run-collapse signature and the e108 self/other readout measure the cost side. The
RMU arm reuses e065's critic-hardened design verbatim.

### First experiments

**e125 — the inversion made explicit (the premise leg; unlearning's warning
label).** On a graduated fact, run the classic removals — address surgery,
address+grown-rows, RMU fine-tune — measured at trained/held-30/novel geometries.
**Registered prediction:** address-level removals leave expression ≥0.85 everywhere
(neutral-or-AMPLIFYING — e115's dimmer read as an unlearning failure mode: cutting
the brake can strengthen the mature memory). Modest, necessary, and it upgrades
T073's side observation to the protocol claim: naive address surgery cannot unlearn
consolidated knowledge.

**e126 — field-dose unlearning (the program's heart).** Graded field ablation
targeted at the fact (remove entries ranked by fact-specific loading — projection
on the fact's content direction — the removal-side twin of e112's forgery probe:
forging failed on summaries; REMOVAL by direction is still testable) vs
mass-matched RANDOM removal. **Registered prediction:** SEGREGATED — fact-targeted
removal collapses the fact at a dose (r·k below the net's share constant) where
random mass-matched removal leaves both fact and run healthy: the mature memory has
its own ~54 units, surgically attackable — the edit law's mature-memory chapter.
WOVEN — the fact only dies inside doses that also break the run (divergence /
attractor entry, the e075 signature): unlearning-is-unselfing — consolidation is the
mechanism by which knowledge becomes inseparable from identity. Either answer is an
arc-closing law; they are not reconcilable, and one rig decides.

**e127 — the consolidation-while-erasing counterwave (T073's lesson as protocol).**
Arm A: N cycles of address-erasure alone (the naive unlearning loop). Arm B:
erasure + field-mass co-ablation at the share boundary each cycle. **Registered
prediction:** A's through-deleted-row expression RISES with cycles (iterated naive
unlearning trains the net to hold the fact harder — T073's trend extrapolated to a
protocol claim); B removes. KILL-side: if A also removes, T073's migration was an
artifact of that specific erase schedule — itself informative, and honored as a
falsification.

### Program vs one-off

PROGRAM either way at e126 — segregation begets a field-surgical unlearning
instrument; coupling begets the unlearning-is-unselfing law (the deepest closure
available: the reason mature memories resist removal is that they are woven into
the self). ONE-OFF only if e125's premise fails (the graduated fact is
address-removable after all — which would contradict e113 and trigger a
replication-crisis check before anything else in the program runs).

### One visualization

**The attack-surface map.** A 2D ablation grid: x = address dose (0 → all grown
rows), y = field dose (0 → past the share boundary); cell color = fact expression;
hatched overlay = run-collapse (self-damage). The mature memory's removal landscape
in one glance: a clean valley of removal inside healthy-run territory (segregated,
surgical) vs the valley existing only inside the self-damage hatch (woven — you
cannot cut the memory without cutting the self).

## 4. P8 — THE RULE'S RESIDUAL: a census of read-policy inversions (e128–e130)

### Organizing question

The read is attention-addressed (AUC 0.907; L1/L2 suffice; 3–4 of 80 coordinates
opened per decision; selection never inspects content — the orthogonal-content
principle). T059's leading reading of the residual: threshold-noise blur where
attention and the age prior disagree — with dp27 standing as "the one genuine
inversion, the personality." One personality is an anecdote; a CENSUS is an object.
**Do inversions across nets and tasks cluster into a SECOND read mode with its own
conditions — an archive-consultation fallback that fires precisely when the
young-band evidence cannot decide — or are they blur? And is a net's inversion rate
a stable fingerprint, like the share constant?**

The fallback hypothesis is the live one because T056 already found the gate's
"second channel lives at near-ties": if the residual read mode concentrates at
low-margin decisions, opens OLD coordinates, and those openings carry content
(opened V aligned with the flip direction — the one place the orthogonal-content
principle could break), then the read policy has a second gear: routing-only
first-order, a rare content-consulting archive read exactly when needed. That would
also speak to why the architecture keeps content orthogonal: it does not throw
content away, it holds it in reserve.

### First experiments

**e128 — the census (eval-only CPU; the instrument exists).** Nets: the six-seed
e098/e117 ladder + the 0.84M family + the e021 COPY net (the one net with TRAINED
far-retrieval — if the second mode is far-retrieval-lite, it should be loudest
there). Machinery: e100's opened-set census. Collect every decision where the rule
opens a coordinate outside attention's top-k; covariates per inversion: margin,
age of the opened coordinate, position, token class, address depth profile, and the
opened V's alignment to the flip direction (the content-vote test).
**Registered prediction:** H-BLUR — inversions sit uniformly in the near-tie
stratum, no covariate separates them from non-inverting near-ties, alignment at
null. H-FALLBACK — inversions concentrate at bottom-quartile margins AND open
disproportionately old coordinates AND show an alignment tail above the e107-class
null. (Pre-register the MC-null guard — e095's lesson — for every concentration
claim.)

**e129 — the fallback probe (deliberate condition construction).** Margin-stratified
battery with the young band degraded at controlled doses (truncate/mask ages ≤ a\*).
H-FALLBACK predicts old-coordinate openings RISE as young-band information falls —
and the decisions stay ACCURATE (the archive read works; a mode that fired and
erred would have been trained away). H-BLUR predicts no rise.

**e130 — the fingerprint rider (free on e128).** Per-net inversion rate vs share
constant / training amount across the ladder: is the residual heritable in the
fingerprint sense (a second per-net parameter beside r\*·k), or does it shrink with
maturity (the rule closing its own gaps)?

### Program vs one-off

PROGRAM if e128/e129 show a structured second mode: its conditions, its accuracy,
its development (does it grow with training? does consolidation of a fact REMOVE
its inversions — the archive entry retiring when the fact graduates?) are the
follow-on arc. ONE-OFF if unstructured: dp27 keeps the title, the footnote writes
itself, and the read-policy card closes at routing-only without remainder.

### One visualization

**The residual atlas.** Per net, a (margin × attention-rank-of-opened-coordinate)
tile with every decision a dot, inversions colored by opened-coordinate age; the
second mode — if real — appears as a distinct old-age cluster at low margin,
side-by-side with a panel of alignment-to-flip distributions (inversions vs null)
showing the content-vote tail. Six nets' tiles in a row turn "personality" into a
population.
