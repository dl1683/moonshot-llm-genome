## R2. The binary cliff and the phases of one substrate

What the compass steers is a type. e147's width ladder was registered as a
graded law (INVARIANCE-CAUSAL: address-key strength A(w) monotone decreasing,
Spearman <= -0.8, route onset NR(w) co-onsetting at the same w*) — it did not
fire (rho -0.381), and the failure is the cleaner finding: A CLIFF, NOT A
DOSE [e147/T087]. At w=1 — the name moving across as few as three read rows —
the row-129 address key is already dead (A: +0.327 locked -> -0.029 at w1;
plateau -0.03..-0.13, no width trend) and novel-geometry expression already
born (NR: 0.071 locked -> 0.696 at w1; 0.903 at w8). The honest magnitude
bound (R46 critic): the step's core is the sign flip and the onset (both
~10-20x their controls); NR decays 0.903 -> 0.605 from w8 to w64 — "no width
trend" overran the numbers, corrected here. The roads diverge in OPPOSITE
directions from the first rung: locked replay strengthens the trained-geometry
key (+0.327) while eroding novel-geometry expression below the untrained root
(0.071 vs 0.166) — massed replay contracts the memory's reach; variance
replay does the opposite on both dials.

**The conversion.** One locked re-teach (300 steps, e143's protocol) at read
rows 183-189 converts the consolidated net [e151/T088]: the site grows
(row-183 strength -0.007 -> +0.512; D-183 now kills half, 0.998 -> 0.486)
while the route door closes EVERYWHERE (g-12 0.916 -> 0.102; g+12 0.948 ->
0.121; D-all 0.905 -> 0.098), the brake dissolves (-0.132 -> +0.005), row-0
strength collapses (0.732 -> 0.106) — at IMPROVED corpus CE (1.6635 -> 1.6490;
mask and ladder instruments replicate e150 on both nets). The committed
TWO-DOOR prediction failed honestly (per-memory types would have kept the old
door open); the failure is the taxonomy's strongest structural statement: the
types are PHASES of one substrate — one net, both phases, bidirectionally
switchable at fixed wiring.

**The dwell, and its correction.** e152 traced the conversion: the cliff runs
between 8 and 16 steps (g-12 retention 0.990 -> 0.447), then DWELLS on a ~0.5
shelf through step 64 — checkpoints 8/32/64 hold BOTH doors (a genuine
row-183-local site-store, 66.8x the bar-mean across dwell peaks, 89x at s8 per
the R47 derivation; site read p_Z 0.962) — before the final descent (0.139 at
s300); the brake OVERSHOOTS mid-conversion (-0.132 -> -0.466 at s128) before

brackets 8->16 / 16->32 / 128->300 across seeds, shelf minima 0.538/0.213/

replicates 3/3: the conversion itself (final <= 0.27 at every seed) and the
brake overshoot (A(129) below -0.35 mid-conversion in 3/3, released by s300)

conversion timing is a washout race — ordinary gradient flow against the

**The conjunction.** e158's 2x2 gives the switch's grammar: jitter@183

committed pass-2]. Two corrections travel with it, named: the R48 critic found

## R4. Split custody: dependence goes one way, the readout the other

**The flat-CE plane (Fig-2 — the honesty centerpiece).** We asked the
registered question — does any row-0-plane intervention remove the

the read column 95.8% at +0.705 — and 0 of 23 bar-eligible cells kill at
<= +0.35. The pre-registered ALL-KILLS-WRECK branch fired, and with it the
reframe: the kill is POISONING, not disconnection. A sub-threshold row-0 norm
(threshold in (0.07, 0.15), sharpened from e141's (0.066, 0.382)) leaves key 0
a value-less mass absorber corrupting downstream reads; the forced-off-sink
mask — all attention to position 0 blocked — SPARES the fact at the
flattest CE of any row-0 intervention ever measured (retention x1.009-1.030 at
+0.03, all three net types). Pre-mask sink attention at the read position is
~0.007/layer: the "route" never carried fact traffic. The near-miss is named:
L0H3-zero alone drops the fact 58.6% at +0.21 — 1.4 points under the kill
bar, report-only. We publish the plane as it landed: the flat-CE fact-kill
does not exist at the sink — the negative is what makes the head-set below

**The killer point.** e160 escalated head surgery: the kill is N2 {L1H0, L0H0}
— WITHOUT the "fact-specific" L0H3 — 79.4%/70.7% (g0, zero/mean modes) at
+0.245/+0.280 CE (NLL3 +0.26); random-4 scatter kills nothing at +0.07-0.13 —

circuit-selective, not type-selective (R47/R48's correction, adopted): the
site-stored memory does not use the shared readout circuit. The unlearning
ordering is now demonstrated with prices: heads > route >> band.

**The double dissociation.** e159: poison + mask jointly heals completely (g0
x1.030, g-12 x1.009 at CE +0.036 — numerically identical to mask-alone; even

g-12, monotone — b=3.0 x0.337, b=4.0 x0.005). MIXED per the R48 correction,
stated as such: healthy content fully rescues at matched conditions;
total-dose absorption kills on a FLATTENED profile; the per-layer
distribution is untested (e167).

**The N2 head-set's mirror — the asymmetry of existence.** e125a pointed the

weakest member); N2 spares the site fact (-0.8%), replicating e160's control

geometry-free, deletion-tolerant — is the one surgery can remove at CE +0.25;
the site-locked memory cannot be excised at any price (scope: head-coordinate

distributed, MLP-heavy state [e153/T091]. Split custody is the summary:
dependence read-coupled to an unremovable pivot; access held by a small,
