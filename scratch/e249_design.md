## The question

The asymmetric map holds (non-P-FIRST => mis-dial 5/5; collapses => P-FIRST 10/10) —
but WITHIN the P-FIRST deaths, what selects the final mode? Pooled: 9 mis-dials vs
7 collapses, all belief-first. T224's registered candidates: (a) the runner-up's
semantic strength (the RU's z); (b) the margin floor (how deep the commitment ground
before the belief crossed); (c) the early-arrival state (does the RU-arrival time
predict the mode?).

## The cell (desk)

For every P-FIRST flip (pooled ~16 records): (1) the RU's z (committed); (2) the
margin trajectory's FLOOR (the minimum margin over states before/at the flip); (3)
the early-arrival state (the first state at the RU, from e247's walker records);

the RU-z distributions; the margin-floor distributions; the arrival-time

## Bars (frozen)

- RU-Z-SELECTS — "the mis-dials' median RU-z exceeds the collapses' by >= 1.5x (or a
  rank test at p <= 0.1, n disclosed) — the neighbor's strength decides the mode:
  strong neighbors catch the falling commitment; weak ones let it fall to the prior"
- MARGIN-FLOOR-SELECTS — "the mis-dials' margin floor sits >= 2x the collapses' —
  the commitment's own remaining depth decides: enough depth to reach the neighbor;
  too little and the prior takes it"
- NEITHER — "no separation — the selector is still unnamed (the honest bound)"
- (Arrival time is a co-report; a bar only if it separates cleanly >= 2x.)
