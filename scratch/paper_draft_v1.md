## Abstract

Char-LMs consolidate facts where their training error sits, not what their stream contains: install-time error placement causally relocates a fact's support and switches its type — a binary cliff at zero-vs-any error-position variance (steering single-lineage). No consolidated state we tested survives continued training (n=3 seeds, 2 families; AdamW at every lr tested); the kill decomposes (PROPOSED, n=3): normalization sets the clock (1683x a matched SGD step; the sign-flip was an estimator artifact), displacement the gate, lethality the trajectory class — a ballistic step kills at 0.92, sign-steps at ~2.5, a diffusive walk grinds on without dying (asymptote unmeasured) — and small displacements strengthen some facts first: the pump is fact-level biography (per-fact n=1). The architectures preceded the physics that explains them: a projection ball holds a fact through the killing wash at a standing tax (n=3 wash draws, one root; the 10M scale cell BLOCKED at the scale-bound recipe stack), and a zero-parameter organ self-times its resurrection (n=3 seeds, one root). Every bar was pre-registered; the correction chain — three rulers bent, two wonder cards dead by their own branches, one projection thrice falsified, one number-pair omitted — is the method's claim: verifiable process, not proof of causality.

## Introduction

What decides whether a memory survives training? The complementary-learning-systems answer is a narrative — replay shuttles memories from a fast, specific store into a slow, distributed one — but the move's mechanism, and what "distributed" means mechanistically, get asserted more often than dissected. We dissect it: character-language models of 0.84-2.7M parameters, raised and consolidated in the lab's own harness, interrogated with pre-registered interventions, deletion batteries, and an adversarial-review loop that fires on schedule whether the news is good. Every interpretation map is committed before compute; every bar that fires, holds, or bends is published where it lands. The account that comes out inverts several intuitions — and the corrections that produced it are part of the result (R5, Fig-3).

The backdrop finding frames everything: these organisms are born with ONE memory organ. The omnipresent row — the attention-sink coordinate — carries every naturally-placed association from first exposure (13/13 install checkpoints: dose ladder, direct and natural installs, re-installs, a fresh family [e142/T085]); the positional "addresses" an earlier arc of this lab dissected are protocol-made grafts, and what we had called migration was share-redistribution around a constant sink-carried core (R3). Scope is stated here, not buried: the compass's steering cells are licensed on ONE lineage (substance 3/3 on a second, the registered specifics draw-brittle [g4R/T134]); the wash law at n=3 seeds over 2 families; the terrain's ORDER at n=3 organisms and 2 facts, its absolute kill distances the fact's strength biography; the pump per-fact n=1; the wall n=3 on a single root (root replicate queued); the rhythm n=3 seeds with timing 2/2 roots and amplitude root-draw-bound; the cone's static rulers n=1 (replication owed); two mechanism cells PROPOSED at n=1 organism/fact/stream [e194/e195]; and the wall's 10M scale cell BLOCKED at the recipe stack — the recipe is scale-bound in all three components (base lr, base steps, consolidation treatment): both licensed lrs U-turn, the inherited schedule overtrains ~3x past its validation minimum, and nothing downstream transfers untested [g1bS/T148]. At 124M, the one external probe (GPT-2) reads as ordinary forgetting with improving perplexity — never a no-basin signature [e182c]. Stamps travel with every claim below: LICENSED, PROPOSED, or BLOCKED, as earned.

The arc runs in five beats. (i) A CAUSAL COMPASS: a fact consolidates where its training error is placed — position, not content — shown by steering the error's site and watching the store follow (R1). (ii) A CLIFF, NOT A DOSE: the compass steers memory TYPE; site-stored and routed memories switch binary at zero-vs-any error-position variance and are PHASES of one substrate, bidirectionally switchable at fixed wiring (R2). (iii) THE WASH LAW: under continued training no consolidated state we tested survives, and the kill decomposes into a clock (optimizer normalization: a fresh AdamW moves 1683x a matched SGD step), a gate (raw displacement), and typed trajectories — annihilation, guillotine, and a diffusive grind that does not die — over a mapped terrain whose ray order g < sign < random is robust while its absolute distances are biography, with a |g|-weighted lethal front and a small-displacement pump that is fact-level biography, not physics (R2b). (iv) SPLIT CUSTODY: content lives everywhere in the body; access differs — dependence read-coupled to an unremovable pivot, the readout held by a small removable head-set, and the memory that generalizes is the memory surgery can remove (R3-R4). (v) THE GENERATIVE TURN: the dissected laws became architecture — a projection ball that walls the front through the killing wash, a zero-parameter organ that self-times its resurrection, a directional forgetting cone (R6).

One framing governs the turn: CONSTRUCTION PRECEDED EXPLANATION. Each architecture was designed from a dissected law, pre-registered with frozen bars before compute, and replicated where licensed; the physics that explains the builds arrived after them. That loop is evidence the laws are causal, not descriptive — and it is not the claim "the same physics is generative" [R6]. Two falsifiers fired honestly inside the program (a pinned single-site wall; a gate-silent detector); two scoped negatives bound it (the compass is positional at the A-floor; single-site walls fail two-site fragility).

The contributions, in order, are boxed at the paper's end.

Memory in these nets is not fragile by necessity but by default. What generalizes from a lab of 2.7M-parameter organisms is not their scale but the checkability of the process: every number traces to a committed artifact, every correction to a registration that predates it. The paper asks to be re-run, not believed.

## R2b. The wash law: a clock, a gate, and typed trajectories

No consolidated state we tested survives continued training under AdamW, at every lr above ~1e-5 within its horizon — licensed, n=3 seeds / 2 families, sparse union of streams [C3; e161-e187; e184; e157; e180]. The kill decomposes into three separable optimizer objects; the terrain beneath is mapped (Fig-5).

**The clock is Adam's arithmetic, not the memory's.** Step-1 pre-clip gradient norm 0.9829 in every arm; a fresh AdamW moves 1.6543 L2/step (lr*sqrt(N), sign-normalized) where matched-lr SGD moves 0.00098 — 1683x at matched lr [opt1/T139]. **The gate is displacement.** Every Adam variant kills at D 2.49–2.84; warmup stretched the step clock 10.08x, the kill still arriving at the same displacement within ~15% (two-convention bracket: checkpoint 2.49 vs 3.64, interpolated 2.18 vs 2.85; the reference arm's registered bar was circular) [opt1/T139 + R58-amendment]. **The pump.** The matched-lr SGD arm ran the same corpus wash with the fact rising (g-12 0.916 -> 0.940–0.955) — the first reading ("the killer stream teaches") was half wrong: the strengthening is a small-displacement PUMP, not an optimizer property (AdamW+warmup pumps to 0.9476 at D 0.368 before dying; SGD lingers at 1/1683rd speed), the claimed sign-flip of the stream's fact-relevance: estimator-point artifact — flattening at a matched point only attenuates (+0.0986 -> +0.0396, ~2.5x); the -0.0385 read is real but post-step [opt1/T139 amendments; e_chart/T150].
