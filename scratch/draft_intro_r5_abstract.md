## Introduction

What decides whether a memory survives training? The
complementary-learning-systems answer is a narrative — replay shuttles
memories from a fast, specific store into a slow, distributed one — but the
move's mechanism, and what "distributed" means mechanistically, get asserted
more often than dissected. We dissect it: character-language models of
0.84-2.7M parameters, raised and consolidated in the lab's own harness,
interrogated with pre-registered interventions, deletion batteries, and an
adversarial-review loop that fires on schedule whether the news is good.
Every interpretation map is committed before compute; every bar that fires,
holds, or bends is published where it lands. The account that comes out
inverts several intuitions — and the corrections that produced it are part
of the result (R5, Fig-3).

The backdrop finding frames everything: these organisms are born with ONE
memory organ. The omnipresent row — the attention-sink coordinate — carries
every naturally-placed association from first exposure (13/13 install
checkpoints: dose ladder, direct and natural installs, re-installs, a fresh
family [e142/T085]); the positional "addresses" an earlier arc of this lab
dissected are protocol-made grafts, and what we had called migration was
share-redistribution around a constant sink-carried core (R3). Scope is
stated here, not buried: the compass's steering cells are licensed on ONE
lineage (substance 3/3 on a second, the registered specifics draw-brittle
[g4R/T134]); the wash law at n=3 seeds over 2 families; the terrain's ORDER
at n=3 organisms and 2 facts, its absolute kill distances the fact's
strength biography; the pump per-fact n=1; the wall n=3 on a single root
(root replicate queued); the rhythm n=3 seeds with timing 2/2 roots and
amplitude root-draw-bound; the cone's static rulers n=1 (replication
owed); two mechanism cells PROPOSED at n=1 organism/fact/stream
[e194/e195]; and the wall's 10M scale cell BLOCKED at the recipe stack —
the recipe is scale-bound in all three components (base lr, base steps,
consolidation treatment): both licensed lrs U-turn, the inherited schedule
overtrains ~3x past its validation minimum, and nothing downstream
transfers untested [g1bS/T148]. At 124M, the one external probe (GPT-2)
reads as ordinary forgetting with improving perplexity — never a no-basin
signature [e182c]. Stamps travel with every claim below: LICENSED,
PROPOSED, or BLOCKED, as earned.

The arc runs in five beats. (i) A CAUSAL COMPASS: a fact consolidates where
its training error is placed — position, not content — shown by steering
the error's site and watching the store follow (R1). (ii) A CLIFF, NOT A
DOSE: the compass steers memory TYPE; site-stored and routed memories
switch binary at zero-vs-any error-position variance and are PHASES of one
substrate, bidirectionally switchable at fixed wiring (R2). (iii) THE WASH
LAW: under continued training no consolidated state we tested survives,
and the kill decomposes into a clock (optimizer normalization: a fresh
AdamW moves 1683x a matched SGD step), a gate (raw displacement), and typed
trajectories — annihilation, guillotine, and a diffusive grind that does
not die — over a mapped terrain whose ray order g < sign < random is
robust while its absolute distances are biography, with a |g|-weighted
lethal front and a small-displacement pump that is fact-level biography,
not physics (R2b). (iv) SPLIT CUSTODY: content lives everywhere in the
body; access differs — dependence read-coupled to an unremovable pivot,
the readout held by a small removable head-set, and the memory that
generalizes is the memory surgery can remove (R3-R4). (v) THE GENERATIVE
TURN: the dissected laws became architecture — a projection ball that
walls the front through the killing wash, a zero-parameter organ that
self-times its resurrection, a directional forgetting cone (R6).

One framing governs the turn: CONSTRUCTION PRECEDED EXPLANATION. Each
architecture was designed from a dissected law, pre-registered with frozen
bars before compute, and replicated where licensed; the physics that
explains the builds arrived after them. That loop is evidence the laws are
causal, not descriptive — and it is not the claim "the same physics is
generative" [R6]. Two falsifiers fired honestly inside the program (a
pinned single-site wall; a gate-silent detector); two scoped negatives
bound it (the compass is positional at the A-floor; single-site walls fail
two-site fragility).

Contributions, in order: (1) the error-placement compass, causal (R1);
(2) the type cliff and the one-substrate phases (R2); (3) the wash law
decomposed — clock, gate, terrain, front, classes — with the biography
pump (R2b); (4) split custody and the asymmetry of existence (R3-R4);
(5) the generative turn — wall, rhythm, cone — construction preceding
explanation (R6); (6) the method itself: pre-registered interpretation
maps, the guaranteed-outcome scan, and the correction chain published as a
first-class result (R5, Fig-3) — verifiable process, not proof of
causality [C9].

Memory in these nets is not fragile by necessity but by default. What
generalizes from a lab of 2.7M-parameter organisms is not their scale but
the checkability of the process: every number traces to a committed
artifact, every correction to a registration that predates it. The paper
asks to be re-run, not believed.

## R5. The correction chain: the method's own story

The practice is pre-registration: every cell ships with an interpretation
map — named branches, frozen bars, out-of-window contingencies split by
side — committed before compute. The map's product is not infallibility;
it is a verifiable record of being wrong fast. Fig-3 gives the timeline
(verdict -> attack -> discriminator -> inversion). This section
inventories the arc's own wreckage.

**Three rulers bent.** The cone's first ruler — the L2 ball — was
guaranteed to spare what it claimed to test (matched-L2 isotropic noise in
~874k dims cannot kill); the cone was rescoped to threshold-ratio form,
organism 4-10x, never again "kills at 1x" [R56-C1; g3K/T137]. Fig-5's
first draft stitched three organisms in mixed currencies — its "sign ray"
was a cumulative path length, its random band another organism's ruler —
stamped UNLICENSED, then re-licensed by the one-organism all-ray map
[R59-K1; e192/T146]. The kill's displacement read was a convention mix
(checkpoint 2.49 vs interpolated 2.18) and the reference arm's registered
bar was circular — its own kill imported as its prediction; both now
travel as brackets, and hypothesis-derived windows are stamped circular at
registration [R58; opt1/T139].

**Two wonder cards died at their own instruments.** W022b's aligned-drift
law was killed by its pre-registered Branch B: death is priced in RAW
displacement (0.6% spread across seeds at matched t*), and the alignment
integral was the wrong currency [e188/T141]. W024's stitches-and-cuts
picture was retired whole by the chart cell it asked for: the pump has NO
cuts — every magnitude class positive [e_chart/T150].

**One celebrated sign-flip dissolved.** "The normalizer flips the sign of
fact-relevance" (cos -0.0385 vs +0.0981 on a bit-identical batch) was an
estimator-point artifact: at a matched point, flattening ATTENUATES
(+0.0986 -> +0.0396); the negative cosine is real but post-step
[e_chart/T150; T139 amendments].

**One projection falsified three times.** The grind's linear asymptote:
projected dead at D~10, then at the s1214 crossing, then by class — walked
through alive each time. The instrument class is retired, not the question
[opt1b/opt1b2/opt1b3; T142/T145/T147].

**One number-pair omitted.** The lethal front's mass fraction reads ~75%
in the machine gate and 86.6% in the committed census; neither reconciles
to an artifact, so the paper quotes no mass number — the kill ladder
stands without it [runs/opt2; T151; R60-audit].

The practice generalizes beyond the inventory. Committed reads supersede
early on-disk reads against a running agent (e158's first fold was
discarded for exactly this — the rule R1 names). Machine records stay
frozen while the interpretive record governs, contradictions documented,
never overwritten (g1bW's adjudication clause). The guaranteed-outcome
scan asks of every gate whether it could fail at all — and the 10M host's
coherence-pass/val-fail split was exactly the gate that could not
[W021; g1bS/T148]. The claim is VERIFIABLE PROCESS, NOT PROOF OF
CAUSALITY: causal standing belongs to the interventions — the steering
plate, the transplants, the necessity instruments — and the chain's own
product is checkability. Every correction above traces to an artifact, a
registration timestamp, and a commit.

## Abstract (final, four sentences)

Char-LMs consolidate facts where their training error sits, not what their
stream contains: install-time error placement causally relocates a fact's
support and switches its type — a binary cliff at zero-vs-any
error-position variance (steering single-lineage). No consolidated state
we tested survives continued training (n=3 seeds, 2 families; AdamW at
every lr tested); the kill decomposes (PROPOSED, n=3): normalization sets
the clock (1683x a matched SGD step; the sign-flip was an estimator
artifact), displacement the gate, lethality the trajectory class — a
ballistic step kills at 0.92, sign-steps at ~2.5, a diffusive walk grinds
on without dying (asymptote unmeasured) — and small displacements
strengthen some facts first: the pump is fact-level biography (per-fact
n=1). The architectures preceded the physics that explains them: a
projection ball holds a fact through the killing wash at a standing tax
(n=3 wash draws, one root; the 10M scale cell BLOCKED at the scale-bound
recipe stack), and a zero-parameter organ self-times its resurrection
(n=3 seeds, one root). Every bar was pre-registered; the correction chain
— three rulers bent, two wonder cards dead by their own branches, one
projection thrice falsified, one number-pair omitted — is the method's
claim: verifiable process, not proof of causality.
